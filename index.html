Model details, privacy, and interpretation

Local inference, unchanged weights.

Xid reads ./best.onnx from this page’s folder. It never edits, retrains, or replaces the model. Photos, videos, and camera frames stay in this browser; the app sends no media to a server. Fonts, the runtime, and the model are downloaded over the network.

What “No ID card” means.

The model was trained on one class, person_with_id_card. “No ID card detected” only means nothing scored above the threshold. The card may be hidden, turned away, too small, or unlike the training images, and a frame with nobody in it gives the same answer.

Learn from predictions, not conclusions.

Confidence is a model score, not a calibrated guarantee. Masks are visualizations, not measurements. Live processing samples frames and may skip frames on slower devices. Never use Xid to grant or deny anyone access.
